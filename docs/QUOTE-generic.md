# Software Development Project Quote

**Prepared for:** Prospective Client
**Prepared by:** Freelance Full-Stack Developer
**Project:** Custom Web Application Development
**Date:** November 20, 2026
**Quote valid until:** December 20, 2026 (30 days)

## Project Description

I am offering a custom web application designed to meet the client's needs. The system will provide a user-friendly interface, responsive design for desktop and mobile devices, and functionality to help users efficiently access and manage information.

### What the Client Will Receive

- A fully functional web application with the agreed-upon features (listed in Appendix A).
- Front-end development with a responsive and user-friendly design.
- Back-end development, including database integration and server-side functionality.
- User authentication and account management, if required.
- Testing to ensure the system works as intended (an automated test suite will be provided and must pass before delivery).
- Deployment of the application to a hosting environment.
- Basic documentation and instructions for using the system.
- 30 days of post-launch bug-fix support (see "Bug Fixes and Maintenance" below).

### Timeline

| Milestone | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Kickoff and requirements sign-off | Week 1 |
| Core functionality complete | Week 6 |
| Demonstration and progress payment | Week 7 |
| Final delivery, walkthrough, and training | Week 8–10 |

Timeline assumes timely client feedback at each milestone; delays in feedback shift the schedule accordingly.

### What Is Not Included

- Custom domain registration and DNS (client provides; setup assistance available at the hourly rate).
- Ongoing hosting and third-party service costs — **the client is responsible for hosting, database, and email service fees after launch** (estimated $5–25/month depending on the provider).
- New features, design changes, or enhancements beyond the agreed scope (quoted separately).
- Content entry (user data, images, copy) beyond what is needed to demonstrate the system.

## Project Price

The total estimated cost for the project is **$3,500**, depending on the final requirements and scope of work agreed upon before development begins.

## Payment Plan

To make the project manageable for both parties, I propose the following payment schedule:

- **30% deposit ($1,050):** Due upon signing the agreement and before development begins.
- **40% progress payment ($1,400):** Due when the main functionality has been completed and demonstrated to the client.
- **30% final payment ($1,050):** Due upon final approval and before the application is officially delivered.

Any additional features or significant changes requested outside the original agreement will be quoted separately and require client approval before work begins.

Payment accepted via ACH, check, or major credit card. Invoices are net-15.

## Bug Fixes and Maintenance

I will provide **30 days of support following final acceptance** (see "Acceptance" below) to address bugs or defects related to the agreed-upon functionality. Bugs discovered during this period will be fixed at no additional cost, provided they are caused by the original development and are not the result of new changes, third-party services, or client modifications.

- **Severity and response targets:** *Critical* (site down or data loss) — response within 24 hours; *Major* (feature broken) — response within 3 business days; *Minor* (cosmetic/edge case) — fixed in the next scheduled release.
- Bugs are tracked in the GitHub issue tracker; the client receives a link to every issue and fix.

Requests for new features, design changes, or enhancements will be treated as additional work and quoted separately.

### After the 30-day support period

- **Hourly rate:** $100/hour, billed in 30-minute increments, for ad-hoc fixes and enhancements.
- **Monthly maintenance plan:** $120/month for up to 5 hours of support, maintenance, and dependency/security updates.
- Fixes for third-party breakage (e.g., an API the app depends on changes its format) are quoted as change requests at the hourly rate.

## Acceptance

Delivery is complete when the client confirms the live application matches the agreed feature list and the automated test suite passes. The client has **10 business days** after the final walkthrough to report acceptance issues; otherwise delivery is deemed accepted.

## Conclusion

My goal is to deliver a reliable, functional, and easy-to-use application while maintaining clear communication throughout the project. This pricing and payment structure helps ensure that both the client and developer understand their responsibilities, project expectations, and costs before work begins.
